About Nagle Analytics

A personal data visualization brand focused on making real-world datasets more visual, interactive, and understandable.

Nagle Analytics is a space for building polished visualizations with a focus on movement, comparison, and storytelling. Projects may include sports analytics, public data, environmental data, maps, dashboards, and visual experiments.

The goal is to create work that is clean, useful, and engaging: visualizations that help people see patterns that would otherwise stay hidden in tables.
