USL Cup Race Tracker.

A dynamic sports analytics project designed to show how teams rise, fall, separate, and shift position as the cup race unfolds week by week.

View tracker preview Back to homepage

Project concept

The USL Cup Race Tracker will visualize weekly standings movement so the season feels alive instead of static.

The final version will focus on team trajectories, rank movement, points progression, and key moments when teams climb, drop, or pull away from the field.

What the final chart should show

  1. Weekly rank

    Where each team sits each week, shown as a movement path across the season.

  2. Points race

    How teams accumulate points and separate from the rest of the field over time.

  3. Momentum

    Which teams are rising, falling, staying steady, or making late pushes.

  4. Interaction

    Team highlighting, tooltips, filtering, and a cleaner way to follow one team through the race.

The real chart comes next.

Once the USL Cup data structure is defined, this preview will become the live interactive tracker for weekly movement, standings, points, and team trajectories.
