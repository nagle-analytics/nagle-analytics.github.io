Tiebreakers will be achieved by the following criteria, in order of importance:

1. Total Wins Against Conference Opponents in League Matches

2. Goal Differential Against Conference Opponents in League Matches

3. Goals Scored Against Conference Opponents in League Matches

4. Head-to-Head Points Earned Against Conference Opponents in League Matches

5. Points Per Game against Conference Opponents in League Matches

6. Points Per Game against Non-Conference opponents in League Matches

7. Number of disciplinary points (i.e., fewest disciplinary points).

8. Lottery conducted by the League.
