# About Nagle Analytics Nagle Analytics is a data visualization brand built around one idea: **Make data movement visible.** I created Nagle Analytics as a place to build and share interactive visualizations, sports analytics, maps, public data stories, and visual experiments. The goal is to take real-world data that might otherwise sit in spreadsheets, reports, tables, or static summaries and turn it into something people can explore, understand, and remember. This site is not meant to be a traditional portfolio. It is a working visualization lab. Here, I will build projects that show how data changes over time, how patterns emerge across geography, how teams rise and fall through a season, how public datasets can tell clearer stories, and how interactive design can make complex information more accessible. ## Who I am I am Jeff Nagle, the creator of Nagle Analytics. My background combines environmental science, public health, veterinary medicine, epidemiology, biostatistics, data analysis, and science communication. I have worked with complex scientific and regulatory datasets, and I am especially interested in the point where data analysis becomes useful communication. Nagle Analytics gives me a space to explore that intersection more broadly: not only through environmental and public health data, but also through sports analytics, maps, dashboards, visual storytelling, and experimental chart design. ## What I build Nagle Analytics focuses on visual projects that make data easier to see and easier to use. Projects may include: * animated sports standings and race trackers * interactive charts that show movement over time * maps that reveal spatial patterns * public data stories * environmental and health-related visualizations * dashboard prototypes * reusable visualization templates * experiments with storytelling, layout, and interactivity The first flagship project is the USL Cup Race Tracker, a dynamic visualization designed to show how teams move through the race week by week. ## Why this exists Data is often presented after the story is already over: as a table, a static chart, or a final report. Nagle Analytics is built around a different idea. I want to show data as something alive: moving, shifting, separating, clustering, accelerating, and changing direction. That is why the site focuses on movement, interaction, and visual storytelling. The goal is not just to make charts look better. The goal is to make patterns easier to recognize and decisions easier to explain. ## What comes next Nagle Analytics will grow project by project. The site will start with the USL Cup Race Tracker and expand into additional sports analytics, maps, public data projects, environmental visualizations, and reusable chart templates. Each project will be a chance to test a new way of making data clearer, more engaging, and more useful.
