What this is

Nagle Analytics is a space for building and sharing interactive visualizations, sports analytics, maps, public data stories, and visual experiments.

The goal is to take data that might otherwise stay buried in spreadsheets, reports, tables, or static summaries and turn it into something people can understand, remember, and explore.

Who I am

I am Jeff Nagle, the creator of Nagle Analytics.

My background combines environmental science, public health, veterinary medicine, epidemiology, biostatistics, data analysis, and science communication.

I have worked with complex scientific and regulatory datasets, and I am especially interested in the point where data analysis becomes useful communication.

Why I am building this

Data is often presented after the story is already over: as a table, a static chart, or a final report.

I want to show data as something alive: moving, shifting, separating, clustering, accelerating, and changing direction.

The goal is not just to make charts look better. The goal is to make patterns easier to recognize and decisions easier to explain.

How projects will grow

  1. Start with a question, dataset, or visual idea worth exploring.
  2. Build a clean first version that communicates the main pattern.
  3. Add interaction, motion, filtering, or storytelling only when it helps.
  4. Publish the result as a visual story, chart demo, map, or reusable template.

First flagship project

The first flagship visualization will be the USL Cup Race Tracker: a dynamic weekly visualization showing how teams move through the race as results accumulate across the season.

It will focus on rank movement, points progression, team momentum, and the visual story of a season unfolding week by week.
