What connects these projects

Analytics is the systematic process of examining raw data to discover meaningful patterns, trends, and insights. Organizations use these insights to optimize operations, reduce costs, and guide strategic decision-making.

Nagle Analytics applies that same idea visually: each project is built to make patterns easier to see, movement easier to follow, and decisions easier to explain.

How the gallery will grow

The gallery is designed as a project hub. The homepage can stay focused and clean, while each visualization, article, or map gets its own page with context, methods, data notes, and the finished interactive work.
